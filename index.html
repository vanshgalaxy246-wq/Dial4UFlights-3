Discover Thira

Where the Aegean meets the sky.

Thira — the island most people know as Santorini — rises from the Aegean Sea in a crescent of whitewashed villages and blue-domed churches, wrapped around one of the world's great volcanic calderas.

Fira, the island's capital, and the cliffside village of Oia are connected by cobbled paths that trace the caldera's edge, opening onto some of the most photographed sunsets in the Mediterranean.

Flights to Thira arrive into Santorini (JTR) International Airport, a short transfer from the caldera villages and the island's beaches of black and red volcanic sand.
